Gatehouse

Build circuits and run real firmware on emulated chips, in your browser or on your desktop.

Download Open the web app

The download is the Windows desktop app. Everything also works in the web app, in a desktop browser.

  • Real firmware

    Your sketch is compiled and runs on an emulated chip, cycle by cycle, not a lookalike.

  • Compiles in the browser

    The compiler runs as WebAssembly on your machine. No server, and it works offline once loaded.

  • A live circuit

    Wire parts on a grid, watch current flow, and probe voltages with a scope and a logic analyzer.
