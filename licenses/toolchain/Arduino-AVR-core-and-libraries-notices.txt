Arduino AVR core 1.8.8 and bundled libraries: licence statements
====================================================================

Shipped in the Gatehouse browser compiler as precompiled archives (core.a,
Wire.a, SPI.a, SoftwareSerial.a, Servo.a, Adafruit_NeoPixel.a, HCSR04.a) and
as header files. The statements below are quoted verbatim from the headers of
the source archives of the release (the files are listed after each block).
Source archives: see SOURCES.txt and the source offer in NOTICE.
